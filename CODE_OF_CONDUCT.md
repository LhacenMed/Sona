# Code of Conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in Sona's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Stay on topic, and keep reports and reviews factual.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what fits the project.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Repeatedly pushing a request after it has been declined.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock threads, close issues and pull requests, and block people from the project, depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it is repeated. Clear harassment leads to an immediate block.

For a single abusive comment you can also use GitHub's **Report content** option in the comment's `···` menu.
